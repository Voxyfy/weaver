Weaver 1.0.0

First release.

  • Dock windows at screen edges or beside each other; the Desktop is a cell that scales its floating windows.
  • Nest splits inside splits; resize from the gap or a window's own edge.
  • Tab groups across apps: drop a window on another's middle and pause.
  • Zoom: double-click a title bar, step in and out, neighbouring cells, back and forward, saved views.
  • Drag previews, parked chips for windows outside the camera, hide or show the Desktop.
  • Settings: gap, edge zone, tab hold, login item, apps to leave alone, every shortcut re-recordable.
  • Arrangements come back after a relaunch.
